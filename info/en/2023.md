Molecules, the basic units of matter, vibrate, rotate, and move. Their motion underlies every interaction and phenomenon in the world, and countless molecules come together to form a single person and a single world. This work begins from the idea that molecules and people, though they exist on different scales, are in fact closely connected.

The three motions and three states of molecules are visualized as particles, and each is expressed through the movement of a dancer. The movements of the particles and the dancer overlap within a single dance film, setting up a visual contrast.

How would it feel if each of our worlds were reduced to a single point, a molecule? The feeling can stretch from the negative, such as simplicity and futility, to the positive, such as preciousness and the power of condensation. The work unfolds the range of these emotions through movement.
